Frequently Asked Questions

Can't find your answer? Get in touch with the team.

General

Who can participate in TechNova?

Any undergraduate or postgraduate student with a valid college ID. A few events have extra eligibility rules, listed on the events page.

Is there a registration fee?

Registration is free for most events. RoboWars and Drone Race have a ₹300 team fee to cover arena and safety costs.

Can I take part in more than one event?

Yes, as long as the timings do not clash. Check the schedule before you register.

Travel and stay

Is accommodation provided?

Shared hostel rooms are available for outstation participants on a first come basis. Tick the accommodation box in the registration form.

How do I reach the campus?

Patiala railway station and bus stand are about 4 km away. Autos and cabs are easily available.

During the event

What should I bring?

Your college ID, laptop and charger for coding events, and your own robot or drone for hardware events.

Will I get a certificate?

Every participant gets an e-certificate. Winners get printed certificates at the prize ceremony.

Who do I contact on the day if something goes wrong?

Find any volunteer in a yellow TechNova T-shirt, or call the coordinators listed on the contact page.
