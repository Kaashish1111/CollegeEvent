About the festival

TechNova started in 2019 as a small coding contest in one lab. Today it brings together students from colleges across North India to build, compete and learn from each other.

This year we have 12 competitions across four categories, hands-on workshops by industry engineers, and a cultural evening to close things out. Whether you write code, solder circuits or just like solving puzzles, there is something here for you.
